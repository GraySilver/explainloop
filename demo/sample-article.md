# From an email invitation to a calendar event

This is a fictional example written for ExplainLoop. It describes a workflow, not a real product integration.

At 09:20, Lin sends a design-review invitation. The email says the meeting is online on Friday, November 6, at 14:00.

An assistant reads the invitation, identifies the meeting time, and creates a calendar event. Once the event is placed, it marks the task as scheduled. The animation follows that order: invitation, time transfer, calendar event, confirmation.

中文摘要：林小姐发来一封周五 14:00 的线上设计评审邀请。示意助手从邮件中识别时间、写入日历，再显示“已安排”。本例为虚构流程。
