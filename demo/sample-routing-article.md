# One coordinator, parallel work

This is an original fictional article written to demonstrate ExplainLoop. It describes a proposed workflow, not a real product or measured result.

A small team receives requests through email, chat, and an issue tracker. A coordinator accepts each request and assigns the research and drafting work to separate specialists. The specialists work in their own contexts, then send their results back to the coordinator. The coordinator combines those results into one reply for the person who asked.

The relationship worth animating is the movement of one request through several roles: sources converge on the coordinator, work branches out, and the reply returns through a single point. The animation does not claim that this workflow has been deployed or that it improves speed.
